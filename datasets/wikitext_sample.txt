 = Valkyria Chronicles III = 

 Senjou no Valkyria 3 : Unrecorded Chronicles , commonly referred to as Valkyria Chronicles III outside Japan , is a tactical role - playing video game developed by Sega and Media.Vision for the PlayStation Portable . Released in January 2011 in Japan , it is the third game in the Valkyria series . Employing the same fusion of tactical and real - time gameplay as its predecessors , the story runs parallel to the first game and follows the " Nameless " , a penal military unit serving the nation of Gallia during the Second Europan War who perform secret black operations and are pitted against the Imperial unit " Calamity Raven " . 
 The game began development in 2010 , carrying over a large portion of the work done on Valkyria Chronicles II . While it retained the standard features of the series , it also underwent multiple adjustments , such as making the game more forgiving for series newcomers . Character designer Raita Honjou and composer Hitoshi Sakimoto both returned from previous entries , along with Valkyria Chronicles II director Takeshi Ozawa . A large team of writers handled the script . The game 's opening theme was sung by May 'n . 
 It met with positive sales in Japan , and was praised by both Japanese and western critics . After release , it received downloadable content , along with an expanded edition in November of that year . It was also adapted into manga and an original video animation series . Due to low sales of Valkyria Chronicles II , Valkyria Chronicles III was not localized in English territories ; but a fan translation compatible with the game 's expanded edition was released in 2014 . Media.Vision would later go on to develop Valkyria Revolution , an action spin - off for the PlayStation 4 and PlayStation Vita . 

 = Tower Building of the Little Rock Arsenal = 

 The Tower Building of the Little Rock Arsenal , also known as U.S. Arsenal Building , is a building located in MacArthur Park in downtown Little Rock , Arkansas . Built in 1840 , it was part of the Little Rock Arsenal during the American Civil War . It is the oldest surviving building in Little Rock , and was the birthplace of General Douglas MacArthur in 1880 . It was designated as a National Historic Landmark in 2011 . 
 The Arsenal was established in the 1830s to store weapons and munitions for the United States Army . Over the years , the site served multiple military and civil purposes before being converted into a city park in the 1890s . The Tower Building has housed various museums and civic institutions throughout the twentieth century . Today , it stands as a testament to nineteenth - century military architecture and early frontier defense . 

 = Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning = 

 Modern deep learning systems rely heavily on large parameter counts to achieve high generalization capability on complex linguistic and visual tasks . However , deploying these multi - billion parameter transformer models onto consumer hardware , edge devices , and mobile platforms presents significant challenges in memory bandwidth , compute capability , and thermal constraints . 
 Model compression techniques such as weight quantization , magnitude and structured pruning , knowledge distillation , and low - rank factorization offer practical paths to dramatically reducing memory footprint while preserving semantic fidelity . AutoMiniLLM provides a systematic framework to profile and benchmark these trade - offs directly on live inference hardware .
